## 2. Main Feature Map & Locations
- **Gaming Journey Timeline**: Located on the **My Journey** tab (bottom navigation). Displays personal game logs, achievements, and custom memories.
- **Match Beacon Lobby**: Located on the **Match Beacon** tab (bottom navigation). Allows seekers to broadcast lobbies or request to join active games.
- **Community Challenges**: Located on the **Challenges** tab (bottom navigation). Shows curated admin tasks and rewards.
- **Notifications**: Located on the **Activity** tab (bottom navigation). Shows comments, reactions, and invite logs.
- **Settings**: Accessible via the gear icon in the top right corner of the **My Journey** tab.

---

## 3. App Store Safety Verification Steps

### A. How to Report User Generated Content
1. Navigate to any timeline card on the **Discover** feed.
2. Tap the **Flag/Report (Alert Triangle)** icon in the top right of the card.
3. Choose or input the violation reason (e.g. Toxicity, Spam, Harassment) and submit.
4. Content is instantly queued to the Moderator Queue dashboard.

### B. How to Block a User
1. Open a user's profile page by clicking their avatar/username.
2. Tap the **Block (Ban)** button next to Follow.
3. Confirming the block will immediately hide their content, remove matching beacons recommendation, and block mutual interaction.

### C. How to Mute a User
1. From any user's profile or card detail, tap the Mute button.
2. The user's comments and activities are suppressed.

### D. Moderator Action Dashboard
1. Log in with a reviewer account (e.g., email containing `admin` or `mod`).
2. Navigate to **My Journey** tab.
3. Tap the **Moderation Shield** icon in the top right header (next to Settings).
4. Review reports, Quarantine content, or Issue Account Warnings.

### E. How to Delete Account
1. Go to **Settings** (Gear icon on **My Journey** tab).
2. Tap **Permanently Delete Account**.
3. Type `delete nexora` in the confirmation input.
4. Tap **Permanently Delete My Data**. All profile credentials and database records are immediately erased.
