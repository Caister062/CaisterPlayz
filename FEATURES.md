# Features for CaisterPlayz

1. **Push Notifications** when someone posts
2. **Reply to Mentions** – easily engage with users who mention you
3. **Badge System** – visible badges for verified users
4. **Quote Posts** – quote and share other users’ posts
5. **Direct Chat** – chat privately with other users
6. **Settings Tabs** – customize notification types, filters, and preferences
7. **Explore Page** – discover content with sections like For You, Trending, Entertainment
8. **Create Lists** – organize and share curated lists of posts or users
9. **Community Creation** – launch and manage your own community
10. **Creator Studio** – tools for creators to earn revenue on CaisterPlayz
11. **Contact CaisterPlayz** – official ways to reach the platform team
12. **Inspiration** – see top posts ranked by engagement for motivation
